# The Elcock Family Light Show

## App support

For help with the app, email [troyelcock4@outlook.com](mailto:troyelcock4@outlook.com). Include your device model, the feature you were using and roughly when the problem happened. Please leave out passwords, verification codes and your home address.

### I can see the lights but can’t hear the music

During an active show, open Listen Live and start playback. Check your internet connection, media volume and selected speaker or Bluetooth device. Look at what is playing in the app to see whether a song has started.

### A show control is unavailable

Snow, Angel Wings and Name in Lights depend on the display being online and the host enabling that feature. A cooldown shows when another visitor has just used it. If the control asks you to be closer, stand near the display and allow location access while using the app. On iPhone, check Settings, Privacy & Security, Location Services and the app’s Precise Location permission.

### My name hasn’t appeared

Open Name in Lights to check your request. Names may wait in the queue or be held for a host to review. An approved name can appear on the outdoor display while the show is running.

### I want to remove my address or account

In Santa tracking, open My House and choose “Forget this house” to clear your address. Under Account, choose “Delete my account” to remove your sign-in and account. Email the show team for help with any remaining records.

## Privacy policy

The Elcock Family Light Show is operated by Troy Elcock in Minchinbury, New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains how the app uses information when you visit the display or take part in a Santa run. Last updated 24 November 2026.

### Accounts and show activities

When you create an account, you provide a name, email address, phone number and password. Google Firebase Authentication handles sign-in. The show service stores your account ID, name and contact details. The organisers use these details to manage your account and contact you about activities such as a winning game entry. If you enable quick sign-in, your device uses its Keychain and biometric unlock. Your fingerprint or face scan stays under your device’s control.

We use guest or account identifiers, requests, times and outcomes to run the lights, enforce cooldowns and troubleshoot problems. If you vote for a song, enter a game or RSVP, we process your selection and the account or session associated with it. Hosts can see event responses and guest counts. Other visitors can see attendance totals and, when game results are revealed, a winner’s display name and winning entry.

Show organisers can upload event photos for visitors to see and audio files for show playback. These selected files are stored on the show service.

### Names on the display

The name you submit enters the show queue. The team can review it and, if accepted, it can appear on the outdoor lights for anyone at the show to see. If AI screening is enabled, the submitted name is sent to Google Gemini to help the team check it. A first name or nickname is enough. Please leave phone numbers, addresses and other private details out of the display text.

### Location and Santa runs

When a show control requires you to be nearby, the app asks for your device location and sends the coordinates, accuracy and time of the reading to the show service to check proximity. Depending on the feature, this can apply to snow, Angel Wings, names and voting.

If you use Santa tracking, the app can send your viewing location or save an address and map coordinates that you choose, to calculate your distance and alerts. Apple Maps helps you look up the address. Other families do not see your viewing location or house on their maps. A volunteer authorised to operate a run can broadcast the Santa vehicle’s location during that run, including while the app is in the background. This is the location that followers see.

### Providers, notifications and audio

Cloudflare hosts the show service and processes network requests, including IP addresses used to limit abuse. If you enable alerts, the service stores device notification tokens and preferences and uses Apple Push Notification service to deliver them. A donation link, when available, takes you to an external fundraiser, which handles any payment information you enter there. The optional speaker-delay test, where available, listens for test tones on your device. The test audio stays on your device.

### Your choices and deletion

You can change app notifications or location and microphone permissions in your device’s Settings. In Santa’s My House screen, choose “Forget this house” to remove the saved address. Under Account, choose “Delete my account” to remove the sign-in, account profile and associated game entries. Use the My House control before deleting your account if you have saved an address.

Show requests, moderation records and operational logs have their own retention settings. Removing a name from the lights does not instantly remove every record of its submission. Email [troyelcock4@outlook.com](mailto:troyelcock4@outlook.com) to ask for access, correction or removal of information, including event responses or records associated with a guest session. Tell us which feature you used and when. Please do not include your password or exact location.
